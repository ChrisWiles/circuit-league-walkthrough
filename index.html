    About this tour

    Captured October 27–29 and November 2–3 and 8–9, 2026 on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator running iOS 26.4, in Dark appearance at the Default app text size. The reference follows several local League examples; setup, waiting searches, booked matches, the one-week time, breaks, team links, scores, boards, recaps and settings were retaken or added on November 2–3 after the League was simplified. Challenges, following and the growth screens were added on November 8, and screens with the new badges, format pictures and pinned next-step buttons were retaken that evening. The Honolulu doubles chapters show earlier examples of matching, rotating results, Team doubles, Scan, replacement scheduling, Pause and a season. Their current behavior and completeness are still being checked. Earlier standalone reference chapters retain their captured identities and dates. People, matches and safety records are fictional local test data. Venue names may refer to real places; the displayed venue details are test data.

    The Your season chapter combines newer local captures with clearly labeled earlier examples, including a confirmed-result transition. Some earlier ranking and statistics screens still need current comparison. This walkthrough publication does not release the Circuit app or backend.
