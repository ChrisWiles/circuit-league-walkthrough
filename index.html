Circuit League walkthrough

The Circuit League is a free, always-open league in the Circuit Pickleball app. Players pick their times and courts, Circuit proposes matches against players at their level, and every confirmed result moves a city leaderboard and goes to DUPR.

This tour follows two fictional players through the iPhone app. Blake is brand new and joins from scratch. Jordan has a week of matches behind them. Tap any screen to see it full size.

About this tour

What you are looking at

How it was captured

  • Screens come from the current app build on an iPhone simulator, against a private test database.
  • Every player, score and message is made up.
  • The leaderboard week was moved back in time so it had enough results to rank players.

Things to know

  • A profile photo is optional. Players without one show their initials.
  • On some screens the test photos failed to load and show as grey circles. In the current app those show the player's initials instead.
  • Contact details are never shown to other players. They talk through match chat.

Not shown here

  • Android, light theme and larger text sizes.
  • The location permission prompt and the on-screen keyboard.
