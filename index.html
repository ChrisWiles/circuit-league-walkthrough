    About this tour

    Captured October 27–29 and November 2–3 and 8–9, 2026 on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator running iOS 26.4, in Dark appearance at the Default app text size. The reference follows several local League examples; setup, waiting searches, booked matches, the one-week time, breaks, team links, scores, boards, recaps and settings were retaken or added on November 2–3 after the League was simplified. Challenges, following and the growth screens were added on November 8, and screens with the new badges, format pictures and pinned next-step buttons were retaken that evening. The Honolulu doubles chapters show earlier examples of matching, rotating results, Team doubles, Scan, replacement scheduling, Pause and a season. Their current behavior and completeness are still being checked. Earlier standalone reference chapters retain their captured identities and dates. Every person, match, venue and safety record is fictional local test data.

    The Your season chapter uses six earlier captures from app revision 016cb496c, including a confirmed-result transition in fictional data. Ranking and statistics code has changed since; a current comparison is pending. This walkthrough publication does not release the Circuit app or backend.
