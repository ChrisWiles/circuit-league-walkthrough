    About this tour

    Captured October 27–29 and November 2–3 and 8, 2026 on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator running iOS 26.4, in Dark appearance at the Default app text size. Every screen comes from one fictional League story; setup, waiting searches, booked matches, the one-week time, breaks, team links, scores, boards, recaps and settings were retaken or added on November 2–3 after the League was simplified. Challenges, following and the growth screens were added on November 8, and screens with the new badges, format pictures and pinned next-step buttons were retaken that evening. Every person, match, venue and safety record is fictional local test data.

    Screens show the state the app was in at capture time, so some show a step in progress, such as a score waiting for the other side. Verification and known gaps · Capture list
