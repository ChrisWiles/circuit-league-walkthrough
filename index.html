Circuit League walkthrough

The Circuit League is a free, always-open league in the Circuit Pickleball app. Players pick their times and courts, Circuit proposes matches against players at their level, and eligible played results contribute to League boards and DUPR submissions.

Fresh iOS screenshots show all seven polish changes first, followed by the wider League flow. Every screen is laid out on this page; tap one to inspect it full size. Previous walkthrough.

What you are looking at

How it was captured

  • These screenshots use the polished local iOS app and an isolated fictional database. This update is not deployed.
  • Every player, score and message is made up.
  • The previous walkthrough is preserved separately, including its older iOS, Android and web evidence.

Things to know

  • A profile photo is optional. Players without one show their initials.
  • Screens show fictional players and initials. No personal contact information is included.
  • Contact details are never shown to other players. They talk through match chat.

Not shown here

  • Light theme, enlarged text, screen-reader gestures and an exhaustive route/overlay/state matrix. Enlarged text was excluded by the owner.
  • Android, physical camera/location, provider delivery, store resumption, published standings, valid stats and ready recaps were not rechecked in this iOS polish pass.
